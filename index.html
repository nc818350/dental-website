Comprehensive Dental Services

From routine cleanings to complex restorative procedures, we offer a full range of dental services to meet all your oral health needs.

General Dentistry

Comprehensive preventive and routine dental care to maintain your oral health.

Routine Cleanings & Exams

Professional dental cleanings and comprehensive oral health examinations every 6 months.

Duration: 60 minutes $120-150

Dental Fillings

Tooth-colored composite and traditional amalgam fillings for cavity treatment.

Duration: 45-90 minutes $150-300

Tooth Extractions

Safe and comfortable tooth removal procedures with sedation options.

Duration: 30-60 minutes $200-400

Fluoride Treatments

Professional fluoride application to strengthen teeth and prevent decay.

Duration: 15 minutes $50-75

Oral Cancer Screening

Comprehensive screening for early detection of oral cancer.

Duration: 15 minutes Included with exam

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance your smile with our aesthetic dental treatments.

Teeth Whitening

Professional in-office and take-home whitening systems for a brighter smile.

Duration: 60-90 minutes $300-600

Porcelain Veneers

Custom-made thin shells to improve tooth appearance and correct minor imperfections.

Duration: 2-3 visits $800-1,500 per tooth

Dental Bonding

Tooth-colored resin to repair chips, gaps, and discoloration quickly and affordably.

Duration: 30-60 minutes $200-500 per tooth

Smile Makeovers

Comprehensive treatment plan to transform your entire smile using multiple procedures.

Duration: Multiple visits Consultation required

Restorative Dentistry

Restore function and appearance of damaged or missing teeth.

Dental Crowns & Bridges

Custom-made caps and bridges to restore damaged or missing teeth with natural appearance.

Duration: 2-3 visits $800-1,500 per crown

Dental Implants

Permanent tooth replacement solution with titanium implants that look and feel natural.

Duration: 3-6 months process $2,000-4,000 per implant

Dentures

Full and partial dentures for multiple missing teeth with comfortable, secure fit.

Duration: 4-6 weeks $1,000-3,000

Root Canal Therapy

Save infected teeth with gentle root canal treatment using modern techniques.

Duration: 60-90 minutes $800-1,200

Orthodontics

Straighten teeth and correct bite issues for improved function and aesthetics.

Traditional Metal Braces

Effective treatment for all types of orthodontic issues with proven results.

Duration: 18-24 months $3,000-6,000

Ceramic Braces

Tooth-colored braces for a more aesthetic appearance during treatment.

Duration: 18-24 months $4,000-7,000

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Nearly invisible removable aligners for discreet orthodontic treatment.

Duration: 12-18 months $3,500-8,000

Retainers

Custom retainers to maintain your new smile after orthodontic treatment.

Duration: 1 visit $300-600
